    How an agent sees a car
    
      
    You see a photo and a price. Your assistant sees the record behind them. When people ask an AI to find, check and book a car, the dealer with the most trusted, queryable record of each car gets the call.
    
      
    New Zealand already has that record. Every dealer listing, online too, must carry a Consumer Information Notice. Here it is as data, pinned to the parts it describes.
    
      
    Connect your assistant
    
      
    Harbour Lane Motors is a fictional demo dealer. Every car on this page is Simulated.
    
    
    
    
    01 · Odometer
    62,140 km, read off the dashboard.
    The notice must give the actual distance travelled, and say if the reading may be wrong. An agent keeps both: the number, and the doubt.
    
    
    02 · WOF and licence
    WOF to April 2027, licence to February 2027.
    Two stickers on the windscreen become two dates. Your assistant can count the months left and warn you before you pay for a car that needs a WOF next week.
    
    
    03 · Cash price
    $33,990, drive-away.
    On the notice the cash price includes GST, registration and licensing. One number to compare, not a starting point.
    
    
    04 · Money owing
    No security interest stated.
    The lien rides on the key. The notice says whether money is owing; it doesn't prove it. So the agent still tells you to search the PPSR on the day you pay.
    
    
    05 · Safety
    Five stars, labelled Simulated.
    Real ratings live on RightCar. These stars are invented, and the chip says so. An agent should never be left guessing which is which.
    
    
    06 · Running cost
    About $34 a week in petrol at 200 km.
    From MBIE's latest weekly pump price, not a guess. Diesels, EVs and plug-in hybrids add road user charges at NZTA's rates.
    
    
    07 · Import history
    First registered in Japan, 2019.
    Imported in 2021, not as a damaged vehicle. Its radio needs a band expander for NZ FM: that's on the notice too.
    
    
    08 · The record
    Then it collapses into data.

          Connect it
          Give your assistant the keys
          
      
          The dealer's endpoint speaks the Model Context Protocol. Add it to Claude or ChatGPT and ask in your own words. It's a demo dealer with Simulated stock: nothing you do there buys, books or holds a real car.
          
    
          https://assembl-lab.vercel.app/api/mcp/Endpoint
          
    
          Streamable HTTP · JSON responses · no sign-in · CORS open · protocol 2026-07-28 and 2025-03-26 to 2025-11-25
          
    
          
      
          Claude
          
        
          1. Open Customize → Connectors, then + Add → Add custom connector.
          2. Name it Agent-ready cars (demo) and paste the URL above. Continue.
          3. Under Authentication choose No sign in, then Add.
          4. In a chat, press + → Connectors and switch it on.
          
        
          Custom connectors are on Free (one connector), Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise. On Team and Enterprise an owner adds it under Organization settings. Claude's help article, checked 25 Oct 2026.
          
      
          ChatGPT
          
        
          1. Go to Settings → Apps → Advanced settings and turn on Developer mode.
          2. Press Create app, give it a name and the URL above.
          3. Choose No Authentication. It appears under Drafts.
          4. Use it in a chat. ChatGPT asks before tools that aren't read-only: book_test_drive and hold_car.
          
        
          Developer mode is on the web for Pro, Plus, Business, Enterprise and Education, and OpenAI marks it as elevated risk. OpenAI's guide, checked 25 Oct 2026. Nothing here is listed in any app directory.
          
      
          What it can do
          
        
            
        
            Try in either: "Is the hybrid RAV4 in Hamilton a good buy at 250 km a week?" or paste a listing you're looking at and ask "is this a good buy?". It never fetches listing sites; it works only from what the chat already has.

            Truth labels on this page

              •

              •

              •

              • 
      
            • Source Copied from an official document: the notice's fields, the checks.
              • 
      
            • Your data Facts you typed or pasted. Sent to this demo's endpoint to run the check; not stored.
