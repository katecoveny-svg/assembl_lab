Agent-ready cars is a demonstration by assembl. Using it, through an AI assistant or on the Agent-ready cars page, means you accept these terms.

What it is, and what it isn't

  • The dealer, Harbour Lane Motors (demo), and all 48 of its cars are Simulated. They're invented for the demonstration. Nothing is for sale.
  • Car images are 3D renders made by assembl, not photographs of real cars.
  • Test-drive and hold requests are Simulated. Nothing is booked, held, sent or paid, and the dealer has no yard, phone or staff.
  • The service never looks a car up by plate or owner, fetches no listing pages, and has no market price data. The car check reads model-level counts (make, model and year) from NZTA's open Motor Vehicle Register.

Information, not advice

Car checks and running costs are general information to help you ask the right questions. They aren't financial, legal or mechanical advice. Running costs combine published figures (MBIE fuel and household power prices, and NZTA road user charges) with stated estimates, and every figure carries its label. Before you buy any car, do the official checks yourself: a PPSR search for money owing, NZTA's WOF and licence check, and an independent pre-purchase inspection.

Your rights under consumer law

Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under New Zealand law that can't be excluded, including under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 and the Fair Trading Act 1986 where they apply.
