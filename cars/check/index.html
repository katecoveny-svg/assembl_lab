      Use it in ChatGPT or Claude
      
        
      Add this connector URL to your assistant, then ask, for example "is a 2016 Aqua with 98k for $11.5k in Auckland a good buy?"
      
        
      https://assembl-lab.vercel.app/api/mcp/
      
        
      Claude
      1. Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector, and paste the URL.
      2. Choose No sign in, then switch it on in a chat with + → Connectors.
      
        
      ChatGPT
      1. Settings → Apps → Advanced settings: turn on developer mode, then Create app and paste the URL.
      2. Choose No Authentication and use it from the composer.
      
        
      It's a demo connector, not listed in either directory. The same connector also has a demo dealer, Harbour Lane Motors, whose cars are Simulated. This check works on any car you describe.
