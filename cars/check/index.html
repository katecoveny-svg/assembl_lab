assembl lab · car check

Is this car a good buy?

Type in the facts from a listing you're looking at. You'll get the check an AI assistant shows when it uses this tool: what the listing should tell you, the red flags, the checks to run before paying, and what the car will cost to run each week.

  • Model facts are live from NZTA's register. How many of this make, model and year are registered in New Zealand, how they came in, and their fuel, engine and rated economy.
  • Plate checks are links to the official free checks: NZTA's WOF and licence check, and a PPSR search for money owing.
  • Nothing you type is stored, and no personal details are asked for. No name, plate, VIN or link.

Runs the car_check tool on assembl's endpoint, the same one ChatGPT and Claude call.

Use it in ChatGPT or Claude

  
Add this connector URL to your assistant, then ask in your own words, for example "is a 2016 Aqua with 98k for $11.5k in Auckland a good buy?"

  
https://assembl-lab.vercel.app/api/mcp/

  

    
Claude
  1. Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector, and paste the URL.
  2. Choose No sign in, then switch it on in a chat with + → Connectors.

    
ChatGPT
  1. Settings → Apps → Advanced settings: turn on developer mode, then Create app and paste the URL.
  2. Choose No Authentication and use it from the composer.

  

  
It's a demo connector, not listed in either directory. The same connector also has a demo dealer, Harbour Lane Motors, whose cars are Simulated. This check works on any car you describe.
