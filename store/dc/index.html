    How this model works

    Each worker and vehicle takes its next job by a fixed rule: pickers build pallets for the store truck that needs them most, order pickers let pallets down to faces before they put new stock away, and forklifts serve the oldest waiting pallet. A job lasts as long as its route divided by its speed, plus its handling times. These are assumptions, not measurements:

    What the model leaves out: vehicles do not block each other, nobody takes a break, and there are no damages, mis-picks or chilled stock.

    What is real here

    The racking, pallets, cartons, totes, roller conveyor, forklifts, order pickers, pallet jacks, dock gates, carts, label printers and safety kit are NVIDIA SimReady 3D models (CC BY 4.0), loaded from web copies in codepawl/simready-glb of nvidia/simready-assets, simplified for the browser. The floor, wall and carton textures come from the same set.

    The building, trucks, wrappers and people are simple models made for this page. The DC, its layout and its stores are fictional; the store routes use real South Auckland suburb names only as places. Every figure in this panel is Simulated.
