    SIMULATED A generic, fictional store, not a real one. Every day is generated from a fixed seed, so the same settings always give the same numbers. Shopper counts, the hourly curve, till speeds, rotas, short-dated volumes, how strongly a markdown lifts sales (30% off sells about 2.2 times as fast), how much a markdown takes from full-price sales (0.35 kg for each reduced kg), what the rescue partner can take, energy by end use, door costs and staff costs are estimates. Door savings leave out the cold-aisle heating that doors also reduce.
