How did that happen? +

A real Rapier soft-body cage drives a smooth 3D surface. Grabbing temporarily pins a small patch; release lets elastic constraints pull it back. The bottom patch stays anchored. Patterns follow the original surface as it deforms. This is an original digital sculpture, not a replica or a physical material test.

No tearing or self-collision is offered. Extreme stretches can fold the surface; Reset shape brings it back. Lighter rendering is the default. Your projects and exports stay local, with no accounts, uploads, camera or microphone.
