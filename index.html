Labels

How to read the labels.

Simulated
A model of a place or a system. The place is invented; any public figures it uses are named on the page.
Shadow
Public data reaches the model each day or month, from Stats NZ, MBIE, Health NZ or the Electricity Authority. Nothing flows back.
Live
A working tool. It makes a real file or object on your device.
Illustrative
Art to play with. It makes no claim about the world.

The browser city combines LINZ bare-earth terrain with a bounded Auckland CBD extract of OpenStreetMap building footprints. Terrain and buildings use ×1 vertical scale. Height tags are not independently verified; mapped floor counts produce explicitly labelled estimates, while unknown heights stay flat. The separate 2D atlas shows streets and footprints. The city AR download uses the same CBD building geometry and a crop of the terrain at uniform ×1 scale. Browser controls and the wider harbour do not carry into AR. The older terrain-only asset remains a separate ×5 model.

Terrain: Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand (CC BY 4.0), national 1 m DEM mosaic, sampled from overviews at approximately 64 m, with a 16 m Rangitoto detail. NZTM2000 horizontal coordinates; NZVD2016 elevations. The browser city and native AR terrain further resample this source to approximately 112 m. Browser city and city AR heights are ×1; the older terrain-only AR relief is ×5. Survey dates vary across the extract; this is not current-condition or survey-grade information. Missing cells are omitted. Water is masked using © OpenStreetMap contributors / OpenMapTiles via OpenFreeMap (ODbL 1.0); low-lying land is retained. Terrain source metadata · City footprint and height sources · City AR scale and crop. None of these views predicts hazards.
