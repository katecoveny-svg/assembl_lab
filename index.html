How to read the labels.

Simulated
A model of a place or a system. The place is invented; any public figures it uses are named on the page.
Shadow
Public data reaches the model each day or month, from Stats NZ, MBIE, Health NZ or the Electricity Authority. Nothing flows back.
Live
A working tool. It makes a real file or object on your device.
Illustrative
Art to play with. It makes no claim about the world.
