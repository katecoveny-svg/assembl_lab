Live and latest

SIMULATED A generic plant sized like New Zealand's largest dryers (about 30 tonnes of powder an hour and 4.4 million litres of milk a day at peak), not any company's site. Monthly milk shares follow DCANZ's national 2022/23 curve (October 13.5% of the year, June 1.1%). Litres per kg of whole milk powder (about 7.5) and milk solids per litre (0.092 kgMS, from 21.0 billion litres and 1.94 billion kgMS in 2024/25) are derived from public figures. Heat use of 5–12 GJ per tonne of powder today, and about 2.9 GJ for an ultra-low-energy design, is from University of Waikato research. Emission factors are estimates: coal 0.109, gas 0.059, wood biomass about 0.002, and electrode boilers on grid power 0.024 tonnes CO₂ per GJ of heat (grid 0.0846 kg/kWh, MfE). Tankers carry about 28,000 litres; a container holds about 22 tonnes of bagged powder. Break-even of about $8.62/kgMS is DairyNZ's 2026/27 estimate. Forecast ranges like $9.50 change through the season.

AI layer: reported results are from the public sources linked in each drawer and in the examples panel, and most are claims by the company or its technology vendor rather than independent audits. Product prices are illustrative, not live Global Dairy Trade results. Dollar figures are New Zealand dollars at an assumed NZ$1 = US$0.58; on the lab site the latest ECB reference rate replaces it, and the latest Global Dairy Trade event and the wholesale power price at Hamilton are shown as context only.
