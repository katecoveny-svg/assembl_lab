Open the fridge door wide and take one photo from the front. Good light helps.

Your photo is read once to list the food below, then discarded. We never see faces, notes on the fridge or names on labels. Nothing is stored, and you can fix every item before it's used, in keeping with the NZ Privacy Act 2020.

All assumptions and sources
        
SIMULATED Prices are Stats NZ average retail prices for September 2026 where available (on the lab site the latest published month replaces them for the same items) (bananas $4.06/kg, apples $4.20/kg, kiwifruit $5.89/kg, oranges $4.45/kg, broccoli $6.37/kg, carrots $2.31/kg, potatoes $3.27/kg, lettuce $4.90, tomatoes $10.19/kg, milk 2 L $5.05, bread 600 g $2.39, eggs $8.37 a dozen, cheese $12.82/kg, butter $7.62, beef mince $24.77/kg); items marked ~ are estimates, including every dinner ingredient not on that list. Where to store each food follows Consumer NZ and Love Food Hate Waste; "use within" days are typical estimates, as NZ sources don't publish them. NZ households waste about $1,364 of food a year, 10.9% of what they buy (Rabobank–KiwiHarvest survey, 2025); vegetables, bread and fruit are most often wasted. We found no reliable evidence of a "best day" for supermarket markdowns, so this plans around freshness instead. The holiday-town premium is your own estimate.
