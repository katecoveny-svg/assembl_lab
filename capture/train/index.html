What runs where

Pose solving uses colmap-wasm (COLMAP and GLOMAP compiled to WebAssembly, BSD licence). Training uses Brush (Apache-2.0) compiled to WebAssembly and WebGPU. Both run in this tab. Training needs Chrome or Edge 134 or later on a Mac or PC with WebGPU subgroups; Safari doesn't offer subgroups yet, so it can't train here.
