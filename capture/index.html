A 3D model you can turn with a finger. The photos you take become one on a computer, after the capture.

Example capture: “Mazda MX5 remastered” by Goran (gorank) on SuperSplat, CC BY 4.0, superspl.at/scene/4271aa24. Cropped, levelled, scaled and compressed. Not made with this page.

How the photos become 3D

Each photo needs to know where it was taken: its pose. Phone apps such as Scaniverse get poses from the phone's own motion tracking. A web page can only do that on Android Chrome with Google Play Services for AR (WebXR). On iPhone and iPad, browsers don't offer it, so this page records sharp photos and the phone's tilt-and-turn sensors, and the training page solves the poses from the photos on your computer. Nothing is uploaded at any step.

Next step

Save the capture, move the file to a Mac or PC (AirDrop, cable or USB stick), and open the training page in Chrome or Edge (assembl capture · train, coming soon).

Fallback: solve poses on your own Mac

If the browser pose solver can't place enough photos, run COLMAP and GLOMAP (both BSD-licensed) on your Mac instead, then load the folder on the training page. The commands are in the module's README, under "Fallback: poses on a Mac".
