All assumptions and sources

SIMULATED A typical 114 m² three-bedroom house, not a specific real one. Heat loss by insulation level and heater efficiencies are engineering estimates; monthly temperatures are NIWA 1991–2020 averages; solar yield (about 1,320 kWh per kW of panels a year in Auckland), solar and battery costs and heat pump efficiency are from EECA; power is priced at 38.3c/kWh including GST, MBIE's all-in average for the year to March 2026; gas at 25.2c/kWh (MBIE, Apr–Jun 2026); solar buy-back 12.7c incl GST. On the lab site a "Live and latest" strip above shows today's wholesale spot price at Ōtāhuhu (Electricity Authority EMI, before lines, retail margin and GST, so not the same as the 38.3c retail price the twin uses), the latest month's generation mix (EMI), the latest weekly petrol price (MBIE) and the weather now (Open-Meteo); none of these change the simulation. An average NZ household uses 6,836 kWh and spends $2,617 a year (MBIE). Your bill depends on your plan and how you live: use this to compare options, then check real quotes.

ESTIMATE FROM YOUR BILL With a bill added, the twin uses your daily charge, your rates and your buy-back, and scales the model house's use to match your bill for the same months. Night rates are assumed to run 11pm–7am and peak 7–11am and 5–9pm on weekdays unless you change them with your retailer. Upgrade costs are typical installed prices, not quotes.
