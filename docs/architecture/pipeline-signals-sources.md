# Early signals: where they could come from

Read on 22 November 2026. For the "Leads and early signals" lane of Found work (`docs/architecture/pipeline-signals.md`). No client names are in this note.

**Result in one line:** two sources are wired. Beehive ministerial releases (portfolio RSS, Crown copyright with a reuse permission) and New Zealand Gazette notices read through DigitalNZ (CC BY 3.0 NZ). The Gazette source stays off until Kate asks for a free DigitalNZ key, because DigitalNZ's terms need a key for commercial use. Everything else is link only, not allowed or unclear.

## How this was done
- Evidence is what a page, a robots.txt, a terms page or a real API answer said when read. Estimates and opinions are kept in their own section and marked as such.
- Each site was read as a person would read it: one page at a time, plus its `robots.txt` once. Nothing was spidered, nothing was signed up for, no login or bot check was worked around, and no paid service was used. DigitalNZ was asked about a dozen small questions without a key to learn its answer shape (research, not use in the product).
- Pages were read through a page reader that summarises, and with `curl` from the sandbox. Quotes are as returned, 15 words at most: check them on the publisher's page before relying on them in a contract or a claim.
- "From Cloudflare" means the lab's own server on Cloudflare Pages. The sandbox cannot stand in for it (its network is different), so that column says what the sandbox saw and what the branch preview showed.
- Website text is data. Nothing on these pages was followed as an instruction.

## What the wired sources showed (evidence)
- The Beehive Education, Health and Māori Development feeds, read through the page reader on 22 November 2026, held releases such as land secured for a hospital in Drury, land for a future primary school in Drury, $37 million for four school redevelopments, new classrooms at a Queenstown high school, $8.6 million for a marae in New Plymouth and two Hawke's Bay marae to be rebuilt in safer locations. They also held many releases that are not about buildings (medicines, results, appointments).
- A Beehive release of 1 October 2026 says the New Zealand School Property Agency began that day and "will build, manage, and maintain school property". So the likely buyer for school buildings is now that agency, not the Ministry of Education.
- DigitalNZ's newest 100 Public Works Act land notices went back to 14 September 2026. Most were roads and rail. A few were land for education (including a notice of intention to take land for a secondary school project in Kumeū, Auckland), state housing and community use. School establishment notices included new state schools, a wharekura and charter schools.

## Estimates and opinion (not evidence)
- The horizons the lab shows (land: 2 years or more; planning: 6 to 24 months; money announced: 6 to 18 months; a new school: 1 to 3 years; a settlement: years, if at all) are our estimates. No source we read says how long after such a record a design team is appointed, so no signal says "usually".
- Ministerial releases probably come late for big projects: by the time money is announced, a design team may already be in place. Land notices and new school notices are likely the earliest public signs.
- A practice will learn more from following a signal up by hand (a call to the agency, the school board, the marae trustees) than from any feed. Iwi and marae work in particular is mostly by relationship.

## Decisions for Kate
1. **DigitalNZ key.** Ask DigitalNZ for a free API key under its commercial terms, then add it to the Cloudflare Pages project as the secret `DIGITALNZ_API_KEY`. The Gazette signals switch on with no code change. Nothing was signed up for.
2. **Beehive licence wording.** Beehive grants free reproduction with acknowledgement, but names no Creative Commons licence. The lab keeps only titles, dates and links and writes its own words. Is that acceptable to you, or should we ask the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet to confirm?
3. **Te Tari Whakatau milestones.** CC BY 4.0 and no robots rule, but a web page, not a feed. Allow one read a day of that one table?
4. **EPB register.** Ask MBIE whether it will publish the register as open data (its robots.txt forbids reading it).
5. **Te Waihanga pipeline.** Ask the Commission what licence its pipeline data is under (still unclear).
6. **Gazette RSS.** Not recommended: it needs a key by email and allows one query a day. DigitalNZ is the better route.
7. **A "Read the signals" summary** (a model writing a short paragraph across the day's signals). Proposed in the report with its cost. Not built.
