Sources, formulas & limitations

Research checked 9 November 2026. Source pages can change.

  • Electricity Authority: Your power bill — daily connection charges and cents per kWh.
  • Inland Revenue: GST — 15%; ex-GST rates are multiplied by 1.15, inclusive rates by 1.
  • EECA: Warmer Kiwi Homes — eligibility depends on owner occupation, pre-2008 home, income-area/card criteria and existing insulation/heating. Check the current form; no grant is included in this calculator.
  • Electricity Authority: Consumer care — support if paying for essential electricity is difficult.

Power = days × daily cents ÷ 100 + Σ(kWh × unit cents ÷ 100), with the chosen GST factor applied to those rates, then inclusive fees minus credits. Money is rounded for display only. Retailer line rounding may differ.

Combined difference = current total − alternative total after the assumed usage reduction − one-off switching cost. Usage reduction is priced at the alternative rates; it is never added again at current rates. Food and school budgets are kept separate because their periods and assumptions differ.

Manual flat/matching-band comparison only. No demand charges, inferred time-of-use splits, solar export, gas, finance or automatic discount rules. Enter known inclusive adjustments explicitly. The kitchen is illustrative; no energy audit, Healthy Homes compliance or mould assessment is provided.

Local storage is accessible to other users of this browser profile. Save is explicit, not automatic. Export downloads a JSON file of your entered numbers; keep it private. No analytics, remote fonts, account data or bill images. Google Maps and fuel-price feeds are not connected in this edition. No network requests send your inputs. Trip data is excluded from saves and exports.

3D kitchen and Instrument Sans are reused from Assembl’s kitchen tool. Third-party asset notices are included with the local assets.
