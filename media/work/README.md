Served from lab.assembl.co.nz because assembl.co.nz is deployed by direct upload, which keeps that site small.

- `waiheke-flythrough.mp4`: a house on Waiheke Island, at dusk. Source: Kate's fal.ai export (HkoGnxvPfIhNTxBlrFQUI_output.mp4, 5 s), re-encoded H.264 1600 px, no audio.

Both are visualisations, not photographs, and are labelled that way on the page. The street address of the Waiheke house is deliberately not used.
