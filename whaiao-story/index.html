    What stays with the right people

      This demonstrator has no publishing controls or access-control system. A live project would need its agreed audience, hosting, model revision, reviewers and permissions configured and tested.

      What we would need to begin
      1. A project and audience Whaiao wants to work with.
      2. An approved model or permission to prepare a web export.
      3. The design choices and options they want to explain.
      4. Named reviewers and agreement on what can be shared.
      5. A review of the browser experience together.
