  • The worlds need JavaScript. Each one also has its own page: race car, claw machine, heart pump, Earth and Moon, milk powder plant, emergency department, grocery warehouse, café.

What each device does

iPhone and iPadSafari or any iOS browser
AR Quick Look opens the world from a file made for it. On your table, pinch to resize. Step inside keeps it at real size: the race car is 5.6 m long, the plant is life size around you.
iPhone has no web AR (WebXR), so the models loop a short animation instead of running the live simulation.
AndroidChrome with Google Play Services for AR
The live world runs in the browser. It builds itself on your table and the model keeps going: tankers queue, patients wait. Step inside puts a doorway on the floor; walk through it.
Without web AR, Google’s Scene Viewer opens the same files as iPhone.
Meta QuestThe Quest browser
Passthrough AR, the same as Android: point the controller at the floor and pull the trigger to place it.
A computerAny modern browser
A 3D preview of each world and a big assembl AR code. Scan it with a phone, or print the card for a classroom wall.

Tested so far: the gallery, every world’s page, its codes and its 3D preview in desktop Chromium; the Android path (placing, the model running, the doorway) in a WebXR emulator; and every iPhone file with Pixar’s USD checker for AR Quick Look. Not yet tried on a real iPhone, Android phone or Quest.

Dairy plant, emergency department and grocery warehouse: 3D assets: NVIDIA SimReady (CC BY 4.0), via codepawl/simready-glb. Every figure in those worlds is Simulated, from each page’s own model.

How it works

  1. 01Shape it

    Everything is built in this tab with three.js. Nothing you make is uploaded.

  2. 02Share the code

    The link carries the object itself: the words, the logo’s outline or the model and its settings. The viewer page rebuilds it from that.

  3. 03Place it

    The phone opens its own AR. Real-world size, so a 6 cm word is 6 cm on your desk and a real-size ute fills the driveway.

What each device does

iPhone and iPadSafari, iOS 13 or later
AR Quick Look. The phone makes a USDZ file from the shape and opens it on a real surface.
Pinch to resize, unless it’s locked to real size. Photo frames ask for a wall.
AndroidChrome with Google Play Services for AR
WebXR in the browser: find a surface, tap to place, drag to turn, pinch to resize.
Without WebXR, library cars open in Google’s Scene Viewer. Other objects download as a GLB.
ComputerAny modern browser
The 3D preview, the code to scan and the files. No camera AR.
GLB opens in Blender and most 3D tools. USDZ opens in Preview and Reality Composer on a Mac.

Tested so far: the studio, the viewer, the files and the codes, in desktop Chromium. Camera AR on real phones hasn’t been tested yet. The AR code follows the same patterns as Google’s model-viewer.

Next steps Proposed

    How many people placed it, and on what device. Not built: this page records nothing.

    Print a code once and change what it shows later. Needs accounts and hosting. Not built.

  • ProposedMore kinds

    Scans, splats and face filters. Not built. (Portals are built for the worlds above, on Android.)
