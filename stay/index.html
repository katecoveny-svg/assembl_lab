    PROPOSED · NOT CONNECTEDarchitecture for discussion

    No live connection to any property's systems exists. Read-only by design: the connector reads reservations, rooms and housekeeping records and never writes rates or availability back. Changing a rate, a restriction or a message template needs the owner's approval, set by the authority levels above.
