    Moving house, said once.

    Sam, an Example tenant, leaves a flat on Calgary Street in Sandringham for a rental across Auckland. Seven agents do the legwork. Every message and form is a draft until Sam says yes, and this sim sends nothing.

      Who does what

      Seven agents, each with a limit.

      Observe, draft, recommend, or act with approval: never more. Nothing leaves without Sam's yes, and in this sim nothing leaves at all.

      • Intentobserverecommend

        Works out what Sam needs, one small question at a time.

      • Searchobserverecommend

        Builds and ranks the shortlist from Sam's answers.

      • Applicationdraftact with approval

        Drafts viewing requests and applications; shares documents only with consent.

      • Tenancyobservedraft

        Reads the agreement, drafts the bond form, keeps the condition record. No tenancy advice.

      • Utilitiesdraftact with approval

        Lines up power and internet from the move-in date and closes the old ones.

      • Movingdraftact with approval

        Plans the move, asks movers for quotes, drafts the change-of-address list.

      • Resolutionrecommenddraft

        Steps in when something goes wrong and finds the next option.
