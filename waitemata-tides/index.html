A LITTLE LOCAL CONTEXT

Understand this tide

Source-grounded notes, using the published LINZ predictions.

What am I looking at?

Choose a date to see its published tide events.

These are predictions. No verified live water-level observation feed is connected. LINZ prediction guidance ↗

Can I use this height at my beach?

These heights apply to Auckland station 070 and its chart datum. They do not provide a measured level at another shoreline or a safe depth for a vessel. Auckland datum ↗

Why can the actual water differ?

Wind and air pressure affect water levels. The animation does not show current weather or currents. Check the Waitematā marine forecast.

Reading these predictions

Times are local to Auckland, including daylight saving. Heights are metres above Auckland chart datum. Its zero is 5.233 m below benchmark DD1N at Captain Cook Wharf. These predictions cover the Auckland station only.

Not for navigation. Actual water levels can differ with wind, air pressure and other conditions.
