Four journeys through one power company

A generic New Zealand electricity retailer, not a real one, seen from eight homes on one street: a household switching in, one moving house, one opening a bill 40% higher than last month's, and one plugging in a new EV. Each journey runs the same nine stages. The part that matters most is the wait, and what the customer sees while they wait.
