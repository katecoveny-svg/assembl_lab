About this page

The seven stage names are the ones the NZ Institute of Architects describes. A task or checklist item that shows a source is assembl’s short wording of what that source says, with a link to the source and the date it was read. Anything marked “assembl starter, not sourced” is assembl’s own wording. No list here is complete: check your council’s current checklist.

This page records what named people said. It does not check a design against any rule, and an approval here is not a signature, a professional sign-off or a council decision.
