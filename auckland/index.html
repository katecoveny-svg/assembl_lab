A map with its workings shown

Real places.
Room for questions.

Coastline, streets, parks and building footprints come from OpenStreetMap via OpenFreeMap vector tiles. This is a fixed Auckland extract, not a live navigation map. Features may be missing or simplified; footprints do not establish height, occupancy or ownership.

The map is north-up in Web Mercator. Distances are approximate at Auckland’s latitude. It shows no flood extents, property risk, safe routes or current conditions. The learning questions are authored by assembl.
