    The data contract: four tables

    PROPOSED · NOT CONNECTEDarchitecture for discussion

    No live connection to any venue's systems exists. Read-only by design: the connector reads sales, rosters and invoices and never writes back to the POS. Changing a roster, a price or an order needs the owner's approval, set by the authority levels above.
