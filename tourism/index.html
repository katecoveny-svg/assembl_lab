Where Aotearoa's visitors go, month by month

Every region's guest nights, rooms and visitor spend, from Stats NZ and MBIE, on a relief map of Te Ika-a-Māui and Te Waipounamu. Play the year and watch February fill the map with light and June drain it. Arcs carry visitors in from each market and out to the regions. Then try a new route, a big event or a currency move, clearly marked as simulated, and see the forecast we froze before the next official release.

    Forecast

    Each month the engine freezes a forecast for the next official release and never edits it. When Stats NZ or MBIE publish, the refresh job fills in the actual and the error. Below the live ledger is the same method run back over past months.
