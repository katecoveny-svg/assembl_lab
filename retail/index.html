    The data contract: five tables

    PROPOSED · NOT CONNECTEDarchitecture for discussion

    No live connection to any store's systems exists. Read-only by design: the connector reads sales, stock, orders and rosters and never writes an order, a price or a roster back. Placing a replenishment order, changing a promotion or publishing a roster needs a person's approval, set by the authority levels above.
