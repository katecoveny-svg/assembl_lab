Questions, answered.

What does assembl do?

assembl is a full-service marketing and advertising agency in Tāmaki Makaurau Auckland. We build websites and campaigns, connect business data and turn drawings into 3D tools. A client can review a room layout, compare a planting estimate or check a reported vehicle position.

What does getting found by assistants mean?

People now ask assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Google who to call. We make your business easy for them to find, understand and recommend: plain facts on your website, structured data, consistent listings, and answers to the questions your customers ask. No one can promise a ranking or a mention, and we won't.

Can you turn a 2D plan into a 3D model or a fly-through?

Yes. We take an architect's plans, as PDF drawings or an IFC or 3D model exported from Revit or ArchiCAD, and build a model to the drawn dimensions. You can share a browser walkthrough so clients can review spaces, furniture and materials together. We can also supply labelled renders and fly-throughs.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the work. After a short conversation we send a written proposal that sets out the scope, price and timing before any work starts.

How do I start?

Use Start a project to get in touch. Send a line about your business and what you'd like to do, and we'll reply to set up a conversation.
