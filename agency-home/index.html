Questions, answered.

What does assembl do?

assembl is a full-service marketing and advertising agency in Tāmaki Makaurau Auckland. We help you get found by assistants and by search, create campaigns across ads, social, film and voice, and show what's possible with working demos, films and 3D twins. A person approves every step.

What does getting found by assistants mean?

People now ask assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Google who to call. We make your business easy for them to find, understand and recommend: plain facts on your website, structured data, consistent listings, and answers to the questions your customers ask. No one can promise a ranking or a mention, and we won't.

Can you turn a 2D plan into a 3D model or a fly-through?

Yes. We take an architect's plans, as PDF drawings or an IFC or 3D model exported from Revit or ArchiCAD, and build a model to the drawn dimensions. Then we render stills and fly-throughs you can share or put online. Every render is labelled as a visualisation, not a photograph.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the work. After a short conversation we send a written proposal that sets out the scope, price and timing before any work starts.

How do I start?

Use Start a conversation in the Contact section. Send a line about your business and what you'd like to do, and we'll reply to set up a conversation.
