Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information assembl collects through assembl.co.nz and lab.assembl.co.nz, why, who we share it with and how you can see or correct it. We follow the Privacy Act 2020.

Who we are

assembl is a marketing agency in Tāmaki Makaurau Auckland. To contact our privacy officer, use Start a conversation on our home page and put "Privacy" in the subject line.

What we collect

  • When you contact us: your name, email address and whatever you choose to send.
  • When you use the guide on this site: the questions you type and the guide's replies. They're sent to our AI provider to write the answer and, if voice is on, the reply text goes to our voice provider to make the audio. We don't keep these conversations.
  • When you use a tool in the lab: plans, models and files you drop into a lab tool stay in your browser. If a tool offers to send a crop of a plan to our AI plan reader, it asks first, and the reader doesn't keep it.
  • When you visit: our hosting provider handles the technical details every website receives, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address.

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and we don't run tracking pixels. Our hosting provider may set a cookie that's strictly needed for security.

How we use it

To reply to you, to provide and invoice the work you ask us for, to run and secure the site, and to meet our legal obligations, such as keeping business records. We won't use your information for anything unrelated without asking you.

Who we share it with

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with the providers that run our business:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site
  • Anthropic, whose Claude models power the guide and the plan reader
  • ElevenLabs, which makes the guide's voice
  • Google, for our email and the site's fonts

These providers are based overseas, mostly in the United States. We use providers that are required, by their terms with us, to protect personal information in ways comparable to the Privacy Act. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep emails and project records for as long as we need them to work with you and to meet our legal obligations. Tax law requires most business records to be kept for seven years. We don't keep guide conversations. Our AI and voice providers process them to produce a reply and may hold them for a limited time under their own terms, for safety and abuse monitoring.

Keeping it safe

We protect the information we hold with reasonable security, including access controls and encrypted connections. If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll tell you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, as the Act requires.

Seeing and correcting your information

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. Contact us through our home page. We'll respond within 20 working days, as the Act requires.

Concerns

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes and show the date at the top.
