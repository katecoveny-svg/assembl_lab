            What is real here

            Real waterfront, fictional night

            • Simulated The night, the guests, the bookings, the kitchen, the messages and every number in the proof. Guests are fictional. No real restaurant, bar or venue is shown or named.
            • Illustrative The Example restaurant: a pavilion and deck drawn on an open stretch of the basin's promenade, where the map has no building. It is not any real business or building.
            • Open data The waterfront: the real coastline, marina basin, wharves and pontoons, building footprints and heights, paths and roads, and the terrain. Heights are true scale. The guests' walking routes are worked out on the real map.
            • Calculated Sunset for Auckland, from the standard solar equations.
            • Weather Checking the live weather feed…
            • Holidays Checking the public holidays feed…
            • Model Where this sits on the lab's maturity ladder: a simulation with no live connection to any restaurant. Weather and holidays arrive as context only; nothing flows back.
