    If you need food, or want to help

    Need food now: Work and Income can help with hardship grants for food. Many schools, churches and community centres run food banks or pātaka kai.

    Have surplus food: food rescue groups such as KiwiHarvest and Fair Food collect from businesses and pass it to charities.

    Waste less at home: Love Food Hate Waste has storage tips and recipes for leftovers. Our kitchen machine plans smaller shops so fragile food gets eaten.
