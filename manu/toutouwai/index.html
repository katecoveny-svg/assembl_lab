Toutouwai

North Island robin · Petroica longipes

An upright, long-legged forest bird that feeds on the ground and stores food to eat later.

Where it lives

  • Mainly the central North Island, from Taranaki to the Bay of Plenty, plus Te Hauturu-o-Toi / Little Barrier Island and Kāpiti Island. DOC has established more populations on predator-free islands, in fenced sanctuaries and at sites with predator control. NZ Birds OnlineDOC
  • Mature forest, tall scrub and mature exotic plantations with a shrub understorey. It favours moist places with an open understorey under a closed canopy. NZ Birds Online

What it eats

  • Mainly invertebrates, from aphids to large prey such as tree wētā, which it beats against the ground or a log. Small berries and fruit in dry late summer and autumn. DOC
  • It forages on the ground a great deal, among leaf litter, and also works over mossy trunks and branches. NZ Birds OnlineTe Ara

Notable adaptations

How its body and behaviour fit the way it lives.

  • Relatively long legs and an erect stance. NZ Birds OnlineDOC
  • Foot-trembling, wing-flicking and tail-flicking flush out hidden prey. NZ Birds Online
  • It stores food it can’t eat yet in crevices, holes or hollows in trees, and comes back for it later. Te Ara adds that the pair steal from each other’s hiding places. DOCTe Ara
  • Where it meets people often, it becomes confiding, and may come within a metre of someone sitting quietly. NZ Birds Online

Conservation status

New Zealand Threat Classification System, 2021

At Risk – Declining

  • CD Conservation Dependent
  • CI Climate Impact
  • PD Partial Decline

Assessed for Petroica longipes. Criterion B(1): 20,000–100,000 mature individuals. NZTCS 2021

Introduced predators (ship rats, possums, stoats and feral cats) take eggs, nestlings and fledglings. Females do all the incubating and brooding, so many are killed on the nest at night. DOC

Birds were last assessed in 2021, and before that in 2016 and 2012. Check the NZTCS database for any newer listing. NZTCS

About this model

  • A stylised procedural reconstruction, not a scan or a measured specimen. Code builds every feather, shaped by eye from field-guide descriptions and photographs.
  • All eight birds are drawn at the same size on screen. In life the toutouwai is about 18 cm long. NZ Birds OnlineDOC See all eight to scale.
  • Every model uses one shared wing shape and the same set of movements (breathing, looking round, hopping, preening). Don’t read wing shape or behaviour from the model.
  • The long legs are only suggested, by raising the body a little. Leg length isn’t measured.
  • The mānuka sprig is stylised too, and not botanically checked.

Question 1 of 3 What does a toutouwai do with food it can’t eat straight away?

Show the answer

Stores it in a crevice or hole and comes back for it later. DOC: uneaten food is stored in crevices, holes or depressions in trees and retrieved later. DOC

Question 2 of 3 What was the toutouwai’s status in the 2021 assessment?

Show the answer

At Risk – Declining. The 2021 New Zealand Threat Classification lists Petroica longipes as At Risk – Declining. NZTCS 2021

Question 3 of 3 Why are female robins especially at risk from predators?

Show the answer

They do all the incubating and brooding, so they are on the nest at night. DOC: because females do all the incubating and brooding, many are killed at night by predators on the nest. DOC
