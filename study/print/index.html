Pre-flight check

    A design process, not just a download

    The learning is in the decisions you make and test. Keep your drawings and design record as evidence of your process.

    1

    Whakaaro · think

    Sketch what you want the model to show. Set your limits: printer bed size, time, material and the thinnest part your printer can make.

    2

    Āhua · form

    Shape the bike, then read the drawing. Which numbers did you choose, and which did the design decide for you?

    3

    Whakamātau · test

    Print it, find one weakness (a snapped spoke, a sagging bar) and change one setting to fix it. Explain what you changed and why.

    Kupu from Te Aka Māori Dictionary: whakaaro (to think, plan), āhua (to form; shape) and whakamātau (to try, test).
