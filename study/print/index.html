What are you learning?

  • Turn a digital design into a physical outcome.
  • Use scale, proportion and constraints to guide decisions.
  • Test a model, identify a weakness and improve it.
  • Keep a drawing and design record as evidence of your process.

Connects to Years 7–10 technological modelling, design and visual communication, and computer-aided manufacturing. For NCEA, use your teacher’s current standard and conditions rather than treating this tool as assessment evidence by itself.

A DESIGN PROCESS, NOT JUST A DOWNLOAD

Whakaaro → āhua → whakamātau.

Think → form → test. Start with a sketch, choose constraints, make a model, then explain what you would change after printing. The learning is in the decisions and the iteration.
