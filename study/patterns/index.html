For teachers · models, curriculum connections and provenance

Suggested exploration for Years 9–10, with wave interference as extension. Rotation connects to spatial reasoning in the NZ mathematics teaching sequence. Arbitrary-angle rotational symmetry and nearest-point regions extend that connection. This is supplementary practice, not a claim to cover an achievement standard or a mandatory unit.

These are deliberately simplified mathematical models. The wave display adds two ideal, equal-amplitude sine waves at one instant; it does not simulate a vibrating plate, audio or water. The distance display uses straight-line distance on a flat plane, without roads or barriers. Patterns are original geometric forms, not representations of customary Māori designs.
