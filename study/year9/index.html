    VISION DETECTIVE
    A camera has data.
    Not understanding.
    Run a real pixel-and-edge analysis on a sample or your own image. Everything happens on your device. This is classical computer vision, not an AI identification service.

    What can you measure?
    Brightness counts light. Contrast describes variation. Edges mark quick changes between neighbouring pixels. None of these measurements can reliably tell you the species of a leaf.
    Why would an AI vision model get it wrong?
    Models learn patterns from training examples. A new angle, unusual light, a rare species or a biased dataset can change the answer. A confident sentence is not proof. Check the source, compare alternatives and ask someone with local knowledge.
    Privacy and Māori data sovereignty
    Do not upload people or private places to unknown services. Māori data can be taonga; decisions about its collection and use need the right people involved. A local whānau, hapū or iwi relationship cannot be replaced by a model.
    
 
    
 
    
  
    AI JUDGEMENT
    Fluent is not the same as true.
    Use the source card to judge three answers. Look for support, missing context and invented certainty. The point is not to catch a robot; it is to strengthen your own reading.
    
  
    SOURCE CARD · CLASSROOM EXAMPLE
    Restoring a local stream
    Students surveyed 40 metres of stream bank. They counted 18 native plants before restoration. One year later, after planting and weed removal, they counted 43. The survey did not measure water quality or fish numbers.
    This is an invented classroom dataset, not a published scientific study.
    
  
    
  
    A four-check habit
    1. Purpose: is the tool helping me learn, or replacing my thinking?
    2. Evidence: which claims can I verify in a trustworthy source?
    3. People: does this use private, cultural or copyrighted material fairly?
    4. Ownership: can I explain and reproduce the final work myself?
    Follow your school’s assessment and AI rules. Generative AI is not permitted in NCEA external assessment.
