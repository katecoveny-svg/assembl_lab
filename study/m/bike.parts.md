# The downhill machine: parts list for the NCEA ski-run steps

The four NCEA steps added to `bike` (L2 AS91171, L3 AS91524) draw a ski run; the Year 9–10 trail, garage and fork are unchanged. Case tint: **peach**.

| # | Part | Real size / value | Material kind, colour | Label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The run (slope profile) | drops 120 m over 400 m of track (sin θ = 0.30, θ = 17.5°); sliders 20–250 m and 100–1000 m | plastic snow, `#F7FAFC` | Stated (pack: Coronet Peak style) |
| 2 | Skier | 72 kg (40–110), reaches the bottom at 34 m/s (5 m/s to the frictionless limit) | painted suit `#E8664A` with a highlight, painted head `#F2B8B8` | Stated |
| 3 | Skis | drawn 30 units long | metal, `#D8DEE8` with a highlight line | Visual |
| 4 | Height and track dimensions | gold vertical for h; text along the slope for the track length and θ | gold line | Exact |
| 5 | Force arrows (forces step) | mg, mg sin θ down the slope, average resistance W ÷ d | slate, coral, teal arrows with heads | Exact for the sliders |
| 6 | Energy bars | Ep lost, Ek at the bottom, friction + air | sand, coral, sky | Exact |
| 7 | Check turn track | radius 10–120 m (drawn at 2.2 px/m, capped) | rubber track `#B9C7D6` with a darker edge | Estimate |
| 8 | Turn forces | needs mv²/r (coral), friction can give μmg (text), entry speed 5–30 m/s, μ 0.1–0.8 (0.5 for an edged ski) | accent arrows | μ Estimate |
| 9 | Berm angle (Level 3) | tan θ = v²/(rg) | text | Exact |

Animation: the skier slides at three times real time with a constant average acceleration a = v²/(2L), so it reaches the bottom at the chosen real exit speed.
