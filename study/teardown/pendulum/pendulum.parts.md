# Pendulum energy: parts and truth

Original teaching rig. No manufacturer dimensions or clock design are reproduced.

The model uses a point mass, massless rod and frictionless fixed pivot. Gravity is 9.81 m/s². Length is pivot to bob centre, 0.30–1.00 m. Release angle is 2–10°. The rod and bob are posed only from the pure model's angle/coordinates. Bearing housing, axle, race and balls are illustrative and can be isolated or exploded; they do not introduce damping or drive.

Small-angle SHM is deliberately used: theta=A cos(phi), omega=sqrt(g/L), v=L thetaDot, U=mgL theta²/2. The plotted potential is this approximation, not the exact mgh. The displayed geometry uses circular bob coordinates. Physical period is labelled separately from the adjustable playback duration, so animation speed is not evidence for a measured period.

The frame, fixed bob size and illustrative density changes are teaching choices. No claim of a functioning pendulum clock. Sources are linked in main.js. Existing study machines are untouched.
