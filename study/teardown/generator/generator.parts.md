# AC generator: model and geometry contract

Original teaching schematic, 1 scene unit = 1 cm. Loop centreline20 cm ×16 cm, hence model area0.032 m².100 electrical turns are represented schematically by five visible, connected decorative turns. Two separate insulated leads rotate with the winding and connect its two ends to separate continuous slip rings. The five drawn turns are not the turn count used in the model. Pole and bench dimensions, brush/ring sizes and lead routing are illustrative. None is a manufactured device specification.

At0° the coil lies in the yz plane, normal+x and field+x. Rotation about+y changes flux as BA cosθ; emf is NBAω sinθ. Resistive load has no inductance; coil has no losses or armature reaction. A steady external drive is assumed. Phase animation intentionally runs slower than the physical RPM input; zero RPM yields zero emf even when inspecting phases.

Inspectable: rotor winding/shaft, north/south field structure, separate continuous slip rings (not DC commutator), carbon brushes, resistor and leads, support frame. No public user-data or AI calls added.
