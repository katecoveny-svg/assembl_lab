03 / Exams

Every exam, year by year.

Free to use. Exam dates are from NZQA's 2026 timetable; check your admission slip, since your school's entries decide what you sit. We link to NZQA for past papers, marking schedules and exemplars instead of copying them, because NZQA restricts reproduction of its assessment material.

Years 9–10 topics follow the refreshed New Zealand Curriculum: English and maths are in use from 2026; science and social sciences are required from 2027, so schools may still teach older content. Your school sets Year 9 and 10 exams.
