All assumptions and sources

A fictional, modern all-electric office, simulated from engineering rules of thumb: LED lighting at 7 W/m², 3 W/m² around-the-clock base load, heat pumps with a seasonal efficiency of 3.2 for heating and 3.5 for cooling, 120 W of equipment per person, and Auckland average temperatures by month. It uses about 85 kWh per m² a year, at the efficient end of the 100 to 300 kWh/m² range quoted for New Zealand commercial buildings. Price: 23 c/kWh excl GST (average commercial price 2024). Carbon: 0.0846 kg CO₂-e per kWh (Ministry for the Environment, 2025 grid average including line losses). Solar: about 1,300 kWh per kW of panels a year in Auckland, $1,600 per kW installed and 10c/kWh for exported power (approximate, check real quotes). Treat the numbers as a model to compare scenarios, not a measured building. Commuting fuel uses 91 petrol at $3.58 a litre (MBIE, week to 16 Oct 2026; on the lab site the latest MBIE week replaces it and is labelled Latest published), about 10 L per 100 km (AA, approximate), 75% of staff driving (estimate), 48 working weeks and 2.31 kg CO₂ per litre.
